# Videogames Hall of Fame

## Adventure

Adventure games are focused on exploration, puzzle-solving and narrative, where the player navigates a story-driven world.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adventure_game)

- [The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time](./adventure/zelda-ocarina.md)

## Fighting

A fighting game is a genre where two characters engage in close-quarters combat, using a variety of moves, combos and special attacks.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fighting_game)

- [Street Fighter II](./fighting/street-fighter-ii.md)

## FPS

A first-person shooter (FPS) is a video game centered on gun fighting and other weapon-based combat seen from a first-person perspective, with the player experiencing the action directly through the eyes of the main character.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First-person_shooter)

- DOOM

## Platformer

A platformer is a genre where the player controls a character jumping and climbing between platforms while avoiding obstacles and enemies.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platform_game)

- Super Mario Bros

## Puzzle

Puzzle video games make up a broad genre of video games that emphasize puzzle solving. The types of puzzles can test problem-solving skills, including logic, pattern recognition, sequence solving, spatial recognition, and word completion. Many puzzle games involve a real-time element and require quick thinking, such as Tetris (1985) and Lemmings (1991).

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puzzle_video_game)

- [Tetris](./puzzle/tetris.md)

## Sandbox

A sandbox game is an open-ended genre that gives players freedom to explore, create and interact with the world without following a fixed set of objectives.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandbox_game)

- Minecraft
